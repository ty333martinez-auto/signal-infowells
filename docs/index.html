Six pillars

No single pillar defines reality alone

These are the six reasoning perspectives—not marketplace plugins. No single pillar is allowed to define reality by itself. Recognition continuously integrates and cross-checks what emerges; whether it is a distinct mechanism remains an open question.

PerceptionWhat is actually here?
ImaginationWhat else is possible?
EmpathyWhat may others see?
IntrospectionWhat is happening within?
EmotionWhat is moving / signaling?
ReasonWhat follows logically?
RECOGNITION

Keep uncertainty visible. Distinguish what is known, believed, suspected, and not yet known. Recognition is not a stamp of certainty and must not be forced to a premature conclusion.

How to understand the framework

A reasoning core, with operational capabilities around it

Use this distinction when onboarding a person or another AI system.

Core: Awareness → Six Pillars ↔ Recognition → Action → Experience → Reflection → Awareness.

Perception examines the outer world; Imagination opens possibilities; Empathy considers another person's perspective; Introspection examines the inner world; Emotion is treated as movement or signal rather than a directive; Reason examines logic.

Recognition is continuous integration and cross-checking. It preserves different levels of knowing rather than manufacturing certainty. The framework's experiments about whether Recognition is distinct from pillar processing remain unresolved.

Operational layer: marketplace skills, governance, orchestration, evidence, validation, provenance, and external task handoffs help run work. They do not redefine the core.

Media handoff

CapCut intent for video tasks

Video creation or editing can produce a portable task another AI or CapCut operator can execute.

When a session detects a genuine video-production need, siw-capcut-intent builds a structured brief with objective, platform, audience, source assets, script, visual style, edit instructions, aspect ratio, duration, audio, captions, deliverable, acceptance checks, and unresolved questions.

The task is recorded under .pmcro/tasks/capcut/ and includes a portable prompt for another AI. Unknowns stay explicit. Generating the intent is not the same as rendering or exporting a video.

python tools/create_capcut_intent.py path/to/task.json
